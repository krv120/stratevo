01 / Product and volume

A quantity without a unit cannot be compared with a supplier's MOQ. Write “Not decided” if you are exploring.

02 / Specifications and customization

A claimed certificate is not verified evidence. Do not enter credentials, sensitive personal data or confidential designs.

03 / Commercial requirements

Freight, taxes, tooling, samples and quality checks may sit outside a unit quote. Unknowns remain unknown in your brief.

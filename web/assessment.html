What to prepare

Your product

Describe its purpose, materials, specifications and customization. Share only documents you have permission to disclose.

Your constraints

Bring a quantity, budget, destination and target date. It is fine if some answers are not settled yet.

Your questions

List uncertainties about samples, certifications, production, inspection and delivery.

Booking takes place on Minup. Opening the calendar does not create an order or a sourcing contract.

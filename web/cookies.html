Application storage inventory

stratevo_session cookie
First-party HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax session cookie for supplier and staff authentication; eight-hour lifetime; Secure on hosted HTTPS. It is sent only to /api routes.
stratevo_supplier_public_v1 localStorage
A backup of the public supplier ID, name, focus and description, saved after successful registration or editing. It contains no private contact fields or authentication credentials. It is reconciled with an authenticated server record, expires after seven days when the application next reads it, and can be removed manually. Storage failure never blocks registration.
Page memory
Unsaved form values, request retry keys and open-menu state last only for the current page. They are not analytics records.

The application has no advertising pixels, third-party analytics, tracking embeds or cookie-consent overlay. Essential sign-in and the requested supplier-account recovery do not depend on accepting marketing cookies. Public browsing does not create a login session.

External links

Minup’s calendar opens only when you follow its link. Its website may use its own cookies and technologies, governed by its notices. No calendar, external fonts or social widgets are embedded here. Vasilis Krevatsoulis can be contacted at info@stratevo.store or +30 6989472740.

Your controls

Sign out to end the browser session. Use Clear Browser Backup in your supplier account or your browser’s site-data controls to remove the public backup. Blocking storage does not prevent server-side registration; blocking cookies prevents authenticated management. If optional tracking is added later, the storage inventory and consent requirements must be reviewed before activation.

What a useful comparison contains

01 / REQUIREMENT

One common specification

Compare the same materials, dimensions, tolerance, finish, packaging, volume and intended use. Record any proposed substitute separately.

02 / COMMERCIAL

More than a unit price

Separate tooling, samples, packaging, freight, taxes and inspection costs. Ask for currency, payment terms, quote validity and the exact delivery term and named place.

03 / EVIDENCE

Claims need context

Record who issued a document, its scope, date and relationship to the actual product and legal business. A logo, marketplace badge or email confirmation is not factory verification.

04 / EXECUTION

Samples before assumptions

Define sample acceptance criteria and what a production quality check would test. An accepted sample alone does not establish the quality of every future unit.

Questions worth answering early

How does a directory differ from a sourcing evaluation?

A directory helps you find listings. An evaluation asks whether an offer fits a defined requirement, what evidence supports it and what remains unverified. A directory listing alone does not answer those questions.

What if I do not know my quantity or budget?

State “Not decided” in your brief and explain your intended use. This makes the uncertainty visible rather than creating an invented target. A meaningful supplier comparison will need a quantity, unit and commercial basis later.

Does a lower MOQ make an offer better?

Not on its own. Check the relevant variant, setup costs, packaging quantities, repeat-order terms and the unit used. Compare like with like; do not convert pieces, sets and cartons without a confirmed pack definition.

What does “verified supplier” actually mean?

Ask what was checked, by whom, on which date and for which legal entity or site. Email verification here proves access to an inbox only. Independent factory verification is not performed automatically by this website or its AI.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

Your reviewed requirements and contact details are saved privately. STRATEVO assesses whether it can help and what needs clarification. Notifications are queued separately; a saved inquiry does not prove email receipt or guarantee a response time. Paid work requires a separate signed scope.

What is included in a sourcing engagement?

Only the services agreed in a separate proposal. Research, supplier checks, sample coordination, inspection and logistics support should be explicitly scoped rather than assumed. This website accepts no orders, payments or binding supplier contracts.

Primary references, not certification claims

Use these references to frame questions; they do not establish a product's compliance or a supplier's suitability.

  • ICC: Incoterms® rules — the owner of the trade-term rules.
  • ISO: certification — distinguish a standard from a third-party certification claim.
  • European Commission: Access2Markets — a starting point for destination-specific import requirements.

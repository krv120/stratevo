Questions worth asking

Does listing a supplier mean it is verified?

No. Finding a name, evaluating commercial fit and independently verifying a factory are different tasks. Profiles contain supplier-provided information. Any paid verification or inspection must have a separately agreed scope.

What should a quotation include?

Specifications and revision, quantity, currency, unit price, tooling, packaging, sample charges, payment terms, lead time, quote validity and delivery responsibilities. Compare totals only after aligning the scope.

How should certifications be checked?

Check the named legal entity, issuing body, certificate number, site, scope and validity. A certificate for one process or site does not establish compliance for every product.

What does MOQ mean?

The minimum order quantity a supplier is willing to accept for a specified product or configuration. Ask whether the minimum applies per material, size, color or total order.

Who signs the purchase contract?

The buyer and supplier. STRATEVO is an independent facilitator and research consultant, not the seller of the goods. Scope and fees for consulting are agreed separately.

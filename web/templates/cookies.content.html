What is not installed

No localStorage tracking identifiers, consent-choice cookie, non-essential analytics, advertising pixels or remote fonts are used by this source. The sourcing assistant dialog is a same-origin iframe, not a third-party embed. External booking and reference sites open only when you choose their links and have their own policies.

Why there is no consent-choice banner here

There are no optional tracking categories to accept in this build. Authentication and requested draft functionality are retained; an empty “Accept all” choice would be misleading. This is an implementation description, not a legal compliance guarantee. The operator must confirm the necessity and legal basis of each feature and inventory any hosting/CDN cookies before launch.

If measurement is introduced later

The current conversion hooks dispatch only in-page stage names. They send no network requests and store no event history. Adding a provider requires separate authorization, a fresh storage/request inventory and any required consent controls before activation.

Who operates this service?

STRATEVO is a sourcing assistance service founded by Billy, operating from Greece. The full legal identity of the data controller, postal address and formal rights-contact arrangements have not yet been confirmed for publication. This is a launch blocker, not a completed legal disclosure.

Contact through a sourcing inquiry. A verified rights-contact process awaits operator confirmation. You can also request a conversation through the booking page.

Customer sourcing inquiries

We store your name, email, optional business/contact details, sourcing requirements, submission time, inquiry source, status and the version of the notice you acknowledged. This enables requested follow-up. Customer inquiries are separate from supplier registrations and are visible only to authorized staff. No marketing subscription is created.

Supplier registration and public products

Business and contact information, registration country/city, optional messaging details and previously retained license PDFs remain private; new PDF uploads are disabled. Product text, photos, advertised prices and MOQs become public after Confirm and Publish (or email confirmation for older pending submissions). Avoid logos, company names, contact details, source links and confidential designs in public product fields. Image metadata is removed, but visible identities cannot be automatically guaranteed absent.

Suppliers can withdraw listings from their account. Publication or email confirmation does not verify a business, certification or product. Internal review is separate from publication.

AI messages

Messages and a bounded recent conversation are sent to the server-configured AI provider to generate an answer. The application does not save chat transcripts in its database. The chat stays in page memory until reset or navigation. If you choose the inquiry handoff, only your own messages are copied into a temporary same-tab draft; you must review them before submitting. That draft expires after ten minutes and is removed when the inquiry form reads it. Contact fields in the inquiry are not sent to the AI.

The operator must confirm the actual provider, processor terms, retention, hosting locations and any international-transfer safeguards before public launch. Do not enter secrets, payment data, sensitive personal information or confidential documents in chat.

Cookies, storage and external services

See the complete application cookie and storage inventory. The optional guided assessment sends no answers to a provider. Its explicit handoff creates a same-tab draft, accepted for ten minutes and removed when the inquiry form reads it. Expired unused drafts can remain until tab closure. Conversion-stage hooks operate only inside the page: no tracking requests or event history are collected.

Essential HttpOnly session cookies support supplier and manager sign-in, with an eight-hour expiry. Browser session storage is used only for an explicitly requested, temporary inquiry draft. There are no implemented analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. Public request limits use pseudonymous connection-address buckets. AI, database hosting, email and booking providers may process data; their contracts, locations and retention must be verified by the operator. Visiting the external booking provider is optional and subject to its own notices.

Email and access

Registration/access emails contain expiring, single-use links. Owner notifications include a record reference and limited sourcing context; staff must sign in to read private details. A saved record or queued email is not proof of inbox delivery. Do not share access links. New PDF uploads are disabled pending malware scanning and quarantine. Existing private documents remain restricted and unscanned.

Lawful basis, retention and your rights

The intended purposes are responding to your requested inquiry, operating supplier accounts and preventing abuse. The appropriate Article 6 lawful bases, any contractual necessity or legitimate-interest assessment, retention periods and deletion/backup procedures require legal review and operator approval. The form acknowledgment is not consent to optional marketing or a substitute for those decisions. Operator-configured retention tooling is disabled until retention periods and execution are explicitly approved. No approved retention schedule is currently represented by this preview.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a supervisory authority such as the Hellenic Data Protection Authority. Use the contact routes above with your record reference; do not send unnecessary identity documents. These requests require human handling, and a verified rights-contact process must be established before launch.

Until these disclosures and operating procedures are completed, this website remains a non-contracting preview. Do not submit unnecessary or sensitive data.

Booking with Minup

Clicking Book a Sourcing Call opens Minup. The calendar is not embedded and is not contacted by this application before you follow the link. Booking details are processed through Minup and used by STRATEVO to arrange the requested meeting. Review Minup’s privacy policy and its own cookie choices.

Storage, service providers and notifications

The hosting/database services store account and listing records. The configured email provider processes sign-in links and notifications; owner notifications can include submitted contact details. Authorized staff may use a configured notification webhook for those details, but never for authentication links. Staff must sign in to access private records. A queued email is not proof of delivery.

A browser backup contains only the public listing, never private contact fields or sign-in credentials. It is used to reconcile your listing with the server after you revisit. It does not restore lost private accounts or make temporary server storage durable. You can remove the backup in your supplier account or browser settings. See the storage notice.

The operator must confirm provider agreements, processing locations and any international-transfer safeguards for the actual hosting configuration. These are not established by the source code. Historical restricted records are not deleted merely by upgrading the website.

Retention and your rights

We retain records while needed for the account or requested sourcing work, complaint resolution and applicable legal obligations; necessity is reviewed when an account closes or a matter is resolved. Deletion must account for documented legal holds and backup cycles. The operator must finalize and apply a specific retention schedule; no automatic permanent-erasure guarantee is made.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where consent applies, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact info@stratevo.store. We may need proportionate identity verification. You may complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority. No marketing subscription is created by registration.

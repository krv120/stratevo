Notice, takedown & review

Report a Listing / IP Infringement. Please provide the exact listing URL and name, a sufficiently detailed explanation of the alleged illegality or inaccuracy, relevant rights and supporting evidence, your name and email, and a statement that the information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge. Do not attach unnecessary personal data. Where the law permits anonymous notices, contact details are not required.

We assess notices without undue delay and remove or restrict content where justified. A report is not automatic proof of infringement. Where contact details are provided, we acknowledge receipt and communicate the decision and available review options. Affected suppliers can request review by emailing the same address with their listing reference and supporting evidence. These requests are handled by a person; a court or competent authority may also provide redress where available.

Time-sensitive safety or illegal-content concerns should be identified clearly. Nothing here limits legal remedies. The operator must maintain an adequately monitored reporting mailbox and apply the procedure consistently.

A practical description of this website and its limits—not a completed set of legally reviewed commercial terms.

What STRATEVO does

STRATEVO helps e-commerce sellers, resellers, dropshippers and small businesses define China sourcing requirements and assess available information and next steps. Submitting an inquiry asks the team to assess whether it can help; it does not guarantee a response time, supplier match or business outcome.

No transaction on this website

No orders, payments or binding supplier contracts are accepted here. Any paid sourcing engagement requires a separate written proposal defining scope, fees and responsibilities before you agree to proceed. This website does not promise free ongoing sourcing or a particular fee.

AI and product information

AI can be wrong. General guidance is not a quotation, inspection, legal opinion or compliance assessment. Supplier-submitted product information is unverified unless specific evidence establishes otherwise. Advertised pricing/MOQs are not binding offers or guarantees of availability, delivery or quality. Do not rely on a listing alone for safety-sensitive or regulated purchases.

Supplier participation

Submitting company/product information does not guarantee a commercial approval, introduction or business. Under the current self-service process, products publish after email confirmation without prior manager approval. Staff may review the registration, request clarification, follow up or remove inappropriate listings after publication. Email confirmation establishes access to an inbox, not business legitimacy.

Publish only content you are authorized to share. Do not include supplier identities, contacts or source links in public product fields; private business fields are separate. Do not upload harmful files, misleading claims or third-party confidential information.

Privacy and unfinished legal disclosures

Read the privacy notice before submitting information. Operator legal identity, full commercial terms, retention procedures and legally appropriate notices remain subject to confirmation and professional review before public launch. No unsupported compliance or consumer-rights waiver is asserted here.

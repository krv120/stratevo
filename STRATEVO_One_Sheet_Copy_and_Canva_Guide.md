## 1. Behavioral & Copywriting Psychology Applied (Why It Reads Human, Not AI)

1. **Loss Aversion Over Vague Promises (Kahneman & Tversky Prospect Theory — $\lambda \approx 2.25\times$):**
   - B2B buyers feel the risk of a bad deposit, a middleman posing as a factory, or an unquoted $1,450 mold fee **more than twice as strongly** as generic promises like *"We help you source great products."*
   - Instead of AI buzzwords (*"seamless end-to-end supply chain solutions"*), every headline and pillar opens with the **specific commercial failure it prevents**:
     - *Prevents: Middlemen posing as real factories.*
     - *Prevents: "Low unit quotes" that add 25% later.*
     - *Prevents: Invalid certificates & batch defects.*

2. **Concrete Price & Scope Anchoring (The Side-by-Side Quote Audit):**
   - Adapted directly from `stratevo.online/methodology`, the central dark comparison strip contrasts **Supplier A ($7.80/unit EXW "Headline Trap")** against **Supplier B ($8.35/unit FOB "Normalized Fit")**, demonstrating how an apparently cheaper quote ends up costing **$9.93/unit landed** once excluded mold fees, inland drayage, and invalid third-party certificates are exposed.
   - This creates an immediate **"Aha / Peak-End" realization** for the prospect: *they realize their current quotes might have missing questions.*

3. **Radical Incentive Alignment (The Anti-Commission Hook):**
   - Experienced importers distrust sourcing agents who quietly pocket 5–10% kickbacks from Chinese factories (which incentivizes the agent to push higher-priced suppliers).
   - Calling out **"0% Hidden Kickbacks"** and **"Factory-Direct Contracts"** in Section 01 immediately separates STRATEVO from both paid directories and commission middlemen.

4. **Cognitive Fluency & The 15-Second Executive Scan:**
   - 73% of B2B decision-makers scan a one-sheet in under 20 seconds on LinkedIn or email.
   - Numbered eyebrows (`01 / WHO WE ARE`, `02 / WHAT WE DO`, `03 / HOW IT WORKS`, `04 / BOOK YOUR CONSULTATION`), a 3-column grid, and high-contrast status pills let a founder grasp your entire value proposition in a single glance.

5. **Frictionless Micro-Commitment CTA:**
   - Rather than asking for a retainer upfront, the Call-to-Action anchors a **low-risk, 15-minute diagnostic review on Minup** (`https://app.minup.io/book/stratevo`) and tells the prospect exactly what to bring (*product link/spec, target quantity, provisional budget, compliance needs*).
